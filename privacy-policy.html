Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GYMatch ("GYMatch", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the GYMatch mobile application and related services.

By using GYMatch, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, password authentication details, and login method.
  • Profile information, such as age, gender, fitness goals, workout preferences, availability, and profile image.
  • Location information, such as your approximate or precise location, when you allow location access, to show nearby gyms and workout partners.
  • App activity, such as saved gyms, active partner status, matches, chat messages, notifications, and gym interactions.
  • Technical information, such as device type, app version, network information, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Information from third-party sign-in providers, such as Google account email and profile information, when you choose Google Sign-In.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create, secure, and manage user accounts.
  • Show nearby gyms and location-based workout partner activity.
  • Display and update user profiles.
  • Enable matching, chat, saved gyms, notifications, and password reset features.
  • Improve app performance, reliability, safety, and user experience.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, fraud, security incidents, or technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

Location Data

GYMatch uses location data to help you discover gyms and workout partners near you. You can control location permissions through your device settings. If you disable location access, some location-based features may not work correctly.

Profile Visibility

Some profile information may be visible to other users when you appear as an active workout partner, match with another user, or participate in app features designed to connect users. Do not add sensitive information to your profile that you do not want other users to see.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following cases:

  • With service providers that help us operate hosting, authentication, email, analytics, diagnostics, maps, and app infrastructure.
  • With other users as needed to support profiles, matching, active partner status, and chat features.
  • With legal authorities if required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

Third-Party Services

GYMatch may use third-party services such as Google Sign-In, map/location providers, hosting providers, and email delivery providers. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account or personal information by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You can update profile information in the app.
  • You can disable location permissions in your device settings.
  • You can stop using active partner or matching features at any time.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

GYMatch is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than where you live. By using GYMatch, you consent to this processing where permitted by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of GYMatch after changes means you accept the updated policy.
